Liberation Serif 2.1.5
Source: https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts
Original, unmodified TTF files from the Debian fonts-liberation package.
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; full notices in COPYRIGHT.txt.

A metrically compatible Times New Roman fallback for devices without Times.
Regular, bold, italic and bold italic use their own original outlines.
CSS keeps Latin text at the regular weight when Chinese uses a medium weight.
This is an exam-style typesetting choice, not an official examination font certification.
