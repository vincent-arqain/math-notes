## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

游客在水上乘船游览，水面漂浮着旅游活动留下的垃圾，形成游览乐趣与环境负担的反差。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：享受公共自然环境时，也应承担保护环境的责任；便利不能以污染为代价。

审题提醒：不要虚构工厂污染或数据；图中问题主要来自旅游行为和垃圾处置。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The drawing shows tourists enjoying a boat trip, while a large amount of rubbish floats on the water nearby. The unpleasant remains of the journey stand in sharp contrast to the pleasure of travel. The picture warns us about irresponsible tourist behavior.
>
> Beautiful places provide enjoyment, but they are also shared environments that need protection. When visitors leave bottles, bags and food waste behind, they damage the scenery and may harm animals living there. If everyone treats a small piece of rubbish as unimportant, the total effect can become serious. Moreover, local people and later visitors have to bear the cost. A pleasant journey should therefore include respect for the place and for the people who depend on it, rather than concern only for our own convenience.
>
> To improve the situation, tourists should carry their waste away and follow local rules. Tour operators should explain these rules clearly and provide proper places for rubbish. Small responsible actions, supported by good management, can help ensure that tourism brings lasting enjoyment instead of lasting damage.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 游客享受游船，水面却漂着大量垃圾；旅行的快乐与旅后残留物形成反差，批评不负责任的旅游行为。

2. 自然美景也是需要保护的公共环境。垃圾损害景观、可能伤害动物；每个人的一点垃圾也会积累，成本由当地人和后来游客承担。

3. 游客带走垃圾、遵守规则；经营者说明规则并提供垃圾处置设施。个人行动与管理共同减少破坏。

## 模板

适用主题：公共生活。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The drawing shows [people enjoying an activity], while [visible damage] appears nearby. This contrast draws attention to the problem of [harmful behavior].
>
> [A shared resource] brings benefits to many people, but it needs protection. When individuals [a careless action], they may harm [affected people or nature]. Although one action may seem small, repeated behavior can lead to a serious problem. For example, [a clear consequence directly linked to the picture]. The costs are often passed on to others. Therefore, enjoying a public resource should also involve taking responsibility for it.
>
> To improve the situation, individuals should [a specific action]. [A relevant organization] should also [a practical measure]. Through personal effort and proper management, we can protect shared resources and make the activity more responsible.

## 填槽说明

- harmful behavior 用名词或动名词，如littering during trips。

- 措施要有执行者：tourists carry waste away，tour operators provide bins。

- 不能把“政府制定法律”当成每题唯一措施；先回应图中具体行为。

## 核心表达

float：漂浮

scenery：风景

bear the cost：承担代价

tour operator：旅游经营者
