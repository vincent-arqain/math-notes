## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

画面以火锅汇聚不同来源的文化元素，用食材相融的形象表达文化之间的交流。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：文化相互交流、借鉴可以丰富生活；开放吸收与珍惜本土传统可以并行。

审题提醒：火锅是文化交融的比喻，不是饮食健康题；无需翻译图中每一个文化专名。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The drawing presents a hot pot containing different cultural elements. Ideas and traditions from various backgrounds appear together as its ingredients. The caption describes this cultural meal as both tasty and nourishing, suggesting that cultural exchange can enrich our lives.
>
> Different cultures offer different ways to understand the world. By learning from them, we can discover new forms of art, new ideas and fresh approaches to familiar problems. At the same time, sharing our own traditions helps others understand us better. For example, a student may enjoy foreign literature while introducing Chinese festivals to international friends. These activities need not weaken local culture. When carried out with respect and thoughtful judgment, they can encourage people to value both their own heritage and the experience of others.
>
> Therefore, we should approach cultural differences with curiosity rather than fear. We can read widely, join cultural activities and explain our traditions clearly. Being open does not mean accepting everything without thought. It means learning what is valuable while preserving what gives our own culture its meaning.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 不同文化元素被放进一口火锅，既美味又有营养的题注说明文化交流能丰富生活。

2. 不同文化带来认识世界的方式；学习外来文化、介绍自身传统可以同时进行。尊重和判断使交流有助于珍惜双方经验。

3. 以好奇心理解差异，广泛阅读、参与活动、介绍传统。开放不等于全盘接受，而是吸收有价值的内容并珍惜自身文化。

## 模板

适用主题：文化与社会。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The drawing uses [a familiar object] to represent [a cultural idea]. Different [elements] appear together, and the details suggest that exchange can bring new value. The image invites us to think about cultural understanding.
>
> Different cultures provide different ways of seeing the world. By learning about [another tradition or activity], we can [a specific benefit]. At the same time, sharing [our own tradition] helps others understand us. For example, [a concrete two-way cultural activity]. Such exchange can encourage mutual respect when people remain open and thoughtful. It does not require us to give up our own identity.
>
> Therefore, we should take part in suitable cultural activities and learn to explain our traditions clearly. We should welcome useful ideas while thinking carefully about them. Openness and respect can help different cultures enrich one another.

## 填槽说明

- 比喻图先交代“什么物体代表什么”，不要把分析写成物体的字面用途。

- two-way 的例子需体现学习别人和介绍自己；不必声称二者完全相同。

- 不会专名就写cultural elements / different traditions，仍可准确描述主旨。

## 核心表达

ingredient：原料、组成部分

nourishing：有营养的；可用good for us

enrich：丰富

heritage：文化传承；可用traditions

## 迁移方法

适用于文化交流、多样性、相互理解。本题是交流融合，2023则重在传统活动的参与和传承。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
