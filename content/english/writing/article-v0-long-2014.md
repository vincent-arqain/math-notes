## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

两幅画面相隔三十年：先是母亲牵着年幼的女儿，后来是成年女儿扶着年迈的母亲。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：照料关系随年龄转换，亲情通过持续陪伴和具体照顾得以延续。

审题提醒：画面明确为母女。不要只讲儿童成长而漏掉成年子女照顾老年父母。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The two pictures show a mother and her daughter at different stages of life. Thirty years ago, the mother held the little girl’s hand. Today, the grown daughter supports her elderly mother as they walk together. Their roles have changed, but the love remains.
>
> The pictures remind us that family care should continue across generations. Parents often spend years helping children grow, and they may need support when they become older. Such support involves more than money. Listening patiently, spending time together and helping with daily tasks can be equally important. For example, a regular phone call may allow an older parent to share worries that would otherwise remain unnoticed. Caring for parents is not merely a duty; it is also a way to maintain a close and trusting relationship.
>
> Therefore, we should make room for family in our busy lives. We can contact our parents regularly, understand their needs and offer practical help while respecting their choices. Love becomes meaningful through ordinary actions, and these small acts can connect different stages of life.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 两图是母女在人生不同阶段的相携：过去母亲牵女儿，现在女儿扶母亲。角色改变，爱延续。

2. 父母帮助孩子成长，年老后也可能需要支持。支持不只是钱，还包括倾听、陪伴和日常帮助；这既是责任，也维系信任关系。

3. 忙碌中为家人留时间，了解需求、提供帮助并尊重选择。爱通过普通的小行动连接人生阶段。

## 模板

适用主题：亲情与教育。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The pictures show [family members] at different stages of life. In the earlier scene, [one person gives care]. In the later scene, [the roles change]. Although time has passed, the bond between them remains.
>
> The contrast reminds us of the value of family care. [People who once offered help] may need support later in life. Such support includes not only [material help] but also [emotional or practical care]. For example, [a specific everyday action and its benefit]. Through these simple actions, family members can understand each other better and maintain a trusting relationship.
>
> Therefore, we should make time for our family and pay attention to their real needs. We can [action one] and [action two], while respecting their choices. Lasting love is shown through regular care, not only through words on special occasions.

## 填槽说明

- earlier / later 对应画面时间变化；不能把先后两图都写成现在同一时刻。

- 物质与情感支持具体化：money / regular calls / help with daily tasks。

- 家庭照顾不是替对方决定一切；结尾尊重需求让论证更完整。

## 核心表达

elderly：年老的

generation：一代人

involve：涉及、包含

maintain：维持

## 迁移方法

适用于孝亲、感恩、代际关系与陪伴。感恩题还需明确回应对方曾给予什么帮助。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
