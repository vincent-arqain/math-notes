## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：推荐。任务：给新到校的澳大利亚教授James Cook 发邮件，推荐你所在城市的若干旅游景点，并给出理由。署名Li Ming，不写地址。所附题面未另列词数，本册按约100词练习。

审题提醒：原题是some tourist attractions，至少写两个景点；称呼用Dear Professor Cook。范文不假定具体城市，用城市博物馆与中央公园作合理设定。

### 合理补充的情境细节

City Museum、Central Park、乘公交到达和下周末陪同参观是范文设定；James Cook教授及推荐多个景点的任务来自题意。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> Dear Professor Cook,
>
> Welcome to our city. I am writing to recommend two places that you may enjoy visiting during your stay.
>
> The first is the City Museum. Its displays offer a clear introduction to local history, so it is a good place to understand the city. I also suggest visiting Central Park, where you can enjoy the lake and watch local people exercise. It is especially pleasant in the morning.
>
> Both places are easy to reach by bus from our university. If you would like some company, I would be glad to show you around next weekend.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

> Dear [name or group],
>
> Welcome to [place]. I am writing to recommend two places that you may enjoy visiting.
>
> The first is [attraction one]. It offers [something to see or learn], so it is a good place to [benefit one]. I also suggest visiting [attraction two], where you can [activity]. It is especially suitable for people who enjoy [interest].
>
> Both places are easy to reach from [starting point]. You may wish to visit them [suggested time]. If you need further information, I would be glad to help. I hope you enjoy your stay.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 中文段意

①欢迎来到本市，推荐两个适合逗留期间游览的地方。②城市博物馆可以帮助了解当地历史；中央公园可以赏湖、观察当地生活，早晨尤其舒适。③从学校乘公交都方便。如果需要陪同，下周末可以带您参观。

## 填槽与改写

1. where 后接“主语＋谓语”，如you can enjoy the lake。

2. interest 用名词或动名词，如nature / walking。

3. 景点名称和路线属于可合理补充的细节；考试不必冒险编造票价、规定等复杂信息。

## 核心表达

display：此处指“展品、陈列”；local：当地的；company：此处指“陪伴”，不是“公司”；show someone around：带某人参观。

## 迁移方法

适用于景点、博物馆、城市活动推荐。记住“地点＋那里能做什么＋为什么适合对方”，不要把书评的情节理由照搬过来。
