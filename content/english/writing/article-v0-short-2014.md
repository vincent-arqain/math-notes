## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：建议。任务：给校长写信，建议如何改善学生的身体状况，补充必要细节。约100词；署名Li Ming，不写地址。

审题提醒：建议应属于学校能实施的措施；不要只对学生说“多运动”，也不要变成投诉校长。

### 合理补充的情境细节

延长体育中心开放时间、组织面向初学者的运动活动，以及散步小组和球类活动，均是范文提出的措施。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> Dear President,
>
> I am writing to offer some suggestions on improving students’ physical health. Many students spend long hours studying and have too little exercise.
>
> First, the university could keep the sports center open longer, especially in the evening. This would allow more students to exercise after class. Second, it would be helpful to organize sports activities that welcome beginners, such as walking groups and friendly ball games. These activities could make exercise easier and more enjoyable.
>
> I hope you will consider these suggestions. With support from the university, more students can develop healthy habits and better balance study and exercise.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

> Dear [name or group],
>
> I am writing to offer some suggestions on improving [situation]. At present, [a specific problem].
>
> First, [the university / your department] could [measure one]. This would allow [people] to [benefit one]. Second, it would be helpful to [measure two]. In this way, [people] could [benefit two] without [a common difficulty].
>
> I hope you will consider these suggestions. With support from [responsible organization], we can [desired result]. Thank you for your time and attention.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 中文段意

①提出改善学生体质的建议：学习时间长、运动不足是需要回应的问题。②学校可以延长体育中心开放时间，并组织适合初学者的活动，使锻炼更方便、更有趣。③希望校方考虑建议，通过学校支持帮助学生形成健康习惯。

## 填槽与改写

1. improving 后用名词短语，如students’ physical health。

2. 每项措施后解释一个直接好处；could / would 使对上级的建议更礼貌。

3. without 后接名词或动名词，如spending too much time。

## 核心表达

physical health：身体健康；allow someone to do：让某人能够做⋯⋯；beginner：初学者；balance A and B：兼顾A与B。

## 迁移方法

适用于改善图书馆、食堂、课程服务等建议信。“问题＋校方措施＋学生受益”比抽象喊口号更容易切题。
