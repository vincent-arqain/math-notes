## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：回复。任务：你在本校Aiding Rural Primary School 项目工作，回复一位国际学生志愿者的咨询邮件，介绍项目细节。约100词；署名Li Ming。

审题提醒：写回复说明，不是再发一封招募通知。围绕农村小学支教写时间、任务和支持措施；不要泛泛介绍学校。

### 合理补充的情境细节

Alex这一称呼、7月5日至12日、附近小学、支教活动、交通培训与衣物准备均是范文补充；Aiding Rural Primary School项目与回复咨询的任务来自题意。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> Dear Alex,
>
> Thank you for your interest in our Aiding Rural Primary School project. I am happy to share some details with you.
>
> The project will run from July 5 to 12 at a primary school near our city. Volunteers will teach simple English, organize reading activities and play games with the children. We will provide transport and a short training session, so previous teaching experience is not necessary. Please bring comfortable clothes and prepare one activity for the pupils.
>
> If you have any further questions, please write to me. We would be delighted to have you on our team.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

> Dear [name or group],
>
> Thank you for your interest in [project]. I am happy to share some details with you.
>
> The project will run from [date] to [date] at [place]. Participants will [task one], [task two] and [task three]. We will provide [support], so you do not need to worry about [a likely concern]. Please bring [necessary items] and prepare [a simple task] before the activity.
>
> If you have any further questions, please write to me. We hope this information is helpful and would be delighted to have you on our team.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 中文段意

①感谢对项目感兴趣，表明这是回复咨询、提供详情。②说明支教日期、地点、教学活动、交通与培训支持，以及志愿者需做的准备。③欢迎继续提问，并表达期待共同参与的意愿。

## 填槽与改写

1. task one 等用动词原形短语；三项按同一种语法结构并列。

2. worry about 后用名词或动名词，如transport / getting there。

3. 原题没有列出咨询问题，范文用时间地点、任务、支持、准备构成完整回复。

## 核心表达

rural：农村的；run：此处指“开展、持续”；transport：交通安排／运输；pupil：小学生。

## 迁移方法

适用于活动详情、志愿项目和工作安排回复。若来信给出具体问题，必须按问题回答，不能只背固定的三条信息。
