## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

一个学生在选课时权衡两类标准：新知识、创新与挑战，或高分、容易通过与较少作业。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：学习选择应重视真实收获和长期能力，避免只图轻松或只看分数。

审题提醒：图中是一位学生面对两类标准，不是两个学生。也不能说课程越难就一定越好。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The picture shows a student choosing courses on a computer. One set of considerations includes new knowledge, creativity and difficulty. The other focuses on high marks, easy exams and little homework. The picture asks us what we really expect from education.
>
> Good marks are useful, but they should not become the only purpose of learning. If we always choose the easiest course, we may miss opportunities to develop new skills and think more deeply. A suitable challenge can help us discover our weak points and improve through effort. For example, a course requiring a small research project may teach us how to find information, organize ideas and solve problems. Of course, choosing wisely also means considering our present ability and workload, rather than seeking difficulty for its own sake.
>
> Therefore, students should judge a course by what they can learn from it, as well as whether they can manage its demands. Teachers can also explain course goals more clearly. Education becomes more valuable when our choices support real learning and long-term growth.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 学生在选课时比较知识、创新、难度与高分、易过、作业少两类标准，问题是教育究竟带来什么。

2. 分数有用，却不该是唯一目的。适度挑战有助于发展能力；研究项目能训练信息收集和解决问题。同时要考虑现有能力与任务量。

3. 选课看学习收获和可承受的要求，教师也应解释课程目标，让选择服务于真正学习和长期成长。

## 模板

适用主题：选择与学习。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> In the picture, [a person] is choosing between two sets of standards. One emphasizes [long-term value], while the other focuses on [immediate ease or reward]. This raises a question about what should guide our choices.
>
> [Immediate reward] can be useful, but it should not be our only goal. If we always [an easy but limited choice], we may miss chances to develop [important abilities]. By contrast, a suitable challenge can help us [a lasting benefit]. For example, [a specific learning or working task and what it teaches]. At the same time, we should consider our abilities and avoid taking on more than we can manage.
>
> Therefore, we should compare choices by their lasting value as well as their practical demands. With clear goals and sensible effort, we can make decisions that support real growth.

## 填槽说明

- 两类标准用one ... while the other ...，但不要因此虚构两个人物。

- long-term value 用名词短语，如new knowledge and useful skills。

- 论证要有边界：适当挑战，不等于拒绝所有容易的课程。

## 核心表达

consideration：考虑因素

workload：工作量／学习任务量

for its own sake：仅为其本身、为难而难

demand：此处指“要求”

## 迁移方法

适用于学习目的、成长与舒适、能力与分数等权衡。可以用在部分职业选择题，但需把课程例子改为工作场景。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
