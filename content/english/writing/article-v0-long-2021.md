## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

孩子喜欢学习戏曲，却担心同学不认同；父亲鼓励他重视自己的兴趣。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：尊重健康的个人兴趣，不因同伴意见轻易放弃；家庭鼓励可以支持独立判断。

审题提醒：不能泛写“父爱伟大”而忽略兴趣与外界评价的矛盾；原图未限定具体剧种，写traditional opera 即可。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> In the picture, a child says that many classmates do not find learning traditional opera interesting. His father replies that enjoying it himself is enough. Their conversation points to the importance of respecting personal interests instead of simply following other people.
>
> We all need advice, but our interests do not have to be the same as everyone else’s. A meaningful hobby can bring pleasure, build skills and give us a reason to keep learning. If we abandon it only because it is unpopular, we may lose something valuable. For example, a student who enjoys painting can learn patience and careful observation through regular practice, even if friends prefer sports. The father’s encouragement also shows how family support can help children become more confident in their choices.
>
> Therefore, young people should listen to reasonable advice while learning to make their own judgments. Parents should respect healthy interests and offer suitable support. We do not need everyone’s approval to enjoy a worthwhile activity; what matters is that it helps us grow and does not harm others.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 孩子担心同学不喜欢自己学习的传统戏曲；父亲认为自己喜欢就够了，强调尊重兴趣而非盲从。

2. 可以听建议，但兴趣无需完全一致。健康爱好带来快乐和技能；仅因不流行而放弃可能失去成长机会。家庭鼓励也有助于建立自信。

3. 年轻人既听合理建议，也学习自主判断；父母尊重健康兴趣。活动有益成长且不伤害他人，就不需要获得所有人的认可。

## 模板

适用主题：选择与学习。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The picture shows [a person] feeling uncertain about [an interest or choice] because of other people’s opinions. [Another person] offers encouragement. The scene suggests that we should respect meaningful personal interests.
>
> Listening to advice is useful, but it does not mean giving up our own judgment. [A healthy interest] can help us [benefit one] and [benefit two]. If we follow others without thinking, we may lose opportunities that suit us. For example, [a familiar case showing the value of an interest]. Support from family or friends can make it easier to continue and learn with confidence.
>
> Therefore, we should consider advice carefully and make choices that match our needs. Others should offer respect and practical support. A worthwhile interest deserves encouragement when it helps us grow and does not harm anyone.

## 填槽说明

- interest 作具体活动时常用动名词，如learning traditional opera。

- 本题不是“完全不听别人意见”，所以保留reasonable advice 的限定。

- 不要把所有个人偏好都写成有益；健康、合理的兴趣才适用此模板。

## 核心表达

abandon：放弃；可用give up

observation：观察

approval：认可、赞同

worthwhile：值得做的；可用useful and meaningful

## 迁移方法

适用于兴趣、自信、独立思考与尊重差异。若图意专门强调传统文化，可补文化价值，但仍要回应同伴评价。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
