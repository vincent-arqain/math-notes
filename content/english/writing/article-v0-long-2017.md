## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

一侧的人以藏书多为满足，另一侧的人正在阅读，并给自己安排了一年读完二十本书的目标。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：拥有书籍不等于获取知识，真正的阅读和实践才使资源产生价值。

审题提醒：不能批评“买书”本身，也不能把数量20误当成所有人必须完成的标准。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The two pictures compare owning books with reading them. In the first, a man is proud of his large collection. In the second, another man is reading and plans to finish twenty books this year. The contrast highlights the difference between having resources and using them.
>
> Books can offer knowledge, but they cannot improve us if they remain untouched. This is also true of online courses, learning apps and other useful tools. Buying or collecting them may make us feel prepared, yet real progress depends on what we actually do. A student who reads ten pages carefully each day may learn more than someone who owns hundreds of books but seldom opens one. Regular action turns possible benefits into real abilities and helps us discover questions that deserve further thought.
>
> Therefore, we should spend less time displaying our resources and more time using them. Choose a suitable book, make a realistic reading plan and take brief notes. The aim is not simply to reach a large number, but to understand, reflect and put useful ideas into practice.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 两幅图对比藏书和读书：一个炫耀收藏，另一个实际阅读并设定计划。重点是“拥有”与“使用”的区别。

2. 书不读就不能帮助人成长；网课、学习软件同理。规律行动才能把资源的潜在价值转为真实能力。

3. 少展示、多使用：选合适的书、定合理计划、做笔记。目标不是凑数量，而是理解、思考和运用。

## 模板

适用主题：态度与行动。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The pictures compare [possessing something] with [using it]. In the first picture, [person one and action]. In the second, [person two and action]. The contrast reminds us that having resources is different from making good use of them.
>
> [A resource] can help us, but its value depends on our actions. If we only [an empty action], we will gain little. By contrast, [a practical action] helps us develop [a useful ability]. For example, [a specific comparison between possession and action]. The point is not to reject useful tools, but to turn their possible benefits into real progress through regular practice.
>
> Therefore, we should choose a clear goal and make a practical plan. Begin with [a small action], check our progress and keep going. What we do with our resources matters more than how much we own.

## 填槽说明

- 开头compare A with B，A、B形式对称，如owning books / reading them。

- a practical action 用动名词，如reading regularly；谓语helps 不变。

- 只能用于“有条件却不行动”的对照，不能生硬套入所有双图题。

## 核心表达

resource：资源

untouched：未被使用的／未动过的

depend on：取决于

put ... into practice：把⋯⋯付诸实践

## 迁移方法

可迁移到行动胜于空想、学习工具的使用、计划与落实。与2020共有“开始做”的论点，本题更强调资源利用。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
