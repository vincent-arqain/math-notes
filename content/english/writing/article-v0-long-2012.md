## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

一个瓶子倒下并流出部分液体，两人面对同一情形，一人只看到损失，另一人注意到仍有剩余。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：同一现实可以引发不同态度；积极态度的价值在于帮助人寻找下一步行动。

审题提醒：不要把图写成“一个满杯与一个空杯”。不能声称乐观能自动消除所有困难。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The drawing shows two people looking at a bottle that has fallen over. Some liquid has spilled out. One person thinks everything is lost, while the other is pleased that something remains. Their different reactions make the message clear: our attitude matters.
>
> In daily life, we often face situations that we cannot change immediately. However, we can choose how to respond. A positive attitude helps us notice what is still possible instead of giving up. For example, after a disappointing exam, a student can either complain about the result or find weak points and improve. The second choice is more likely to lead to progress. Being positive does not mean ignoring problems; it means facing them with hope and a practical plan.
>
> Therefore, we should learn to look for a useful next step when difficulties arise. We can ask what remains, what can be improved and who can help. When hope is supported by action, a difficult experience may become a chance to grow. This approach is useful in both study and daily life.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 图中两人看着倒下的瓶子：一人认为全完了，另一人庆幸还有剩余。反应的差别说明态度很重要。

2. 我们不能立即改变所有处境，却能选择回应方式。积极态度使人看到行动空间。例如考差后寻找薄弱点比抱怨更有用。积极不等于无视问题，而是带着希望和计划面对问题。

3. 遇到困难时寻找有用的下一步，看看还拥有什么、能改善什么、可以向谁求助。希望与行动结合，困难也可能成为成长机会。

## 模板

适用主题：态度与行动。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The drawing shows [people] facing [the same situation]. One person [negative reaction], while the other [positive reaction]. Their different reactions suggest that our attitude can influence the way we deal with difficulties.
>
> In daily life, we cannot always control [a difficulty], but we can choose how to respond. If we only [unhelpful action], we may lose the chance to improve. By contrast, [helpful action] allows us to [direct benefit]. For example, [a specific example of a problem and a response]. A positive attitude does not remove every problem, but it helps us find a useful next step.
>
> Therefore, we should [action one] and [action two]. Rather than waiting for everything to become easy, we can begin with [a small step]. With hope and steady effort, we are more likely to make progress.

## 填槽说明

- negative reaction / positive reaction 要填第三人称单数谓语，如gives up / looks for a solution。

- helpful action 用动名词短语，如learning from mistakes，与后面的allows 保持单数一致。

- 例子至少写“遇到什么困难＋怎么做”，不能只填for example, students。

## 核心表达

spill out：洒出

positive attitude：积极态度

weak point：薄弱之处

ignore：忽视

## 迁移方法

适用乐观、面对失败、自信等态度类题。核心是“态度改变行动”，不是无条件宣称积极情绪保证成功。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
