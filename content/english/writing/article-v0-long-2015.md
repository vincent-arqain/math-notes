## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

四个人坐在同一张餐桌旁，却各自把注意力放在手机上，面对面的交谈受到冷落。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：手机使用失衡可能削弱面对面交流；应让工具服务于人与人的联系。

审题提醒：不能说手机本身没有价值，也不要虚构“图中所有人都玩游戏”等细节。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> The picture shows four people sitting around a table filled with food. Instead of talking to one another, each is looking at a mobile phone. They are physically together, yet their attention is elsewhere. The scene reveals a problem in the way we use technology.
>
> Mobile phones make communication convenient, but they can also interrupt the people closest to us. A meal with friends offers a chance to listen, share experiences and understand feelings. If we spend the whole time checking messages, that chance may be lost. The problem is not the device itself, but our failure to decide when to use it. For example, putting a phone on the table and checking it repeatedly may make a friend feel that the conversation does not matter. Attention is an important part of respect.
>
> Therefore, we should set simple limits during meetings and meals. Putting phones away, turning off unnecessary notices and listening carefully are useful steps. Technology should support our relationships, and we should remain able to give the person in front of us our full attention.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 四人同桌聚餐，却都看手机：身体在一起，注意力却不在现场，反映技术使用问题。

2. 手机便利交流，也可能打断身边的人。聚会本可倾听和分享，却可能被看消息替代。问题在于使用方式，注意力也是尊重的一部分。

3. 聚会与吃饭时设定简单限制：收起手机、关闭无关提醒、认真倾听，让技术服务关系。

## 模板

适用主题：公共生活。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The picture shows [people] together, but they are all busy with [a device or distracting activity]. Although they share the same space, they pay little attention to one another. The scene suggests a problem of imbalance.
>
> [A useful tool] brings convenience, but excessive use may weaken [an important human activity]. When we [an unsuitable action], we can lose the chance to [a meaningful interaction]. For example, [a small everyday case and its effect on another person]. The problem is not that the tool has no value. Rather, we need to decide when and how to use it so that it serves our real needs.
>
> Therefore, we should set clear limits in [a relevant situation]. We can [action one] and [action two]. By using technology thoughtfully, we can enjoy its benefits while protecting the relationships that matter.

## 填槽说明

- excessive use 是“过度使用”，避免写成all use。

- relevant situation 可填family meals / conversations with friends。

- 本题写通知提醒可用notices / notifications；不熟生词时写turn off the phone sound。

## 核心表达

physically：身体上、实际位置上

interrupt：打断

device：设备

attention：注意力

## 迁移方法

适用于手机依赖、网络与现实交流、技术使用边界。保持“承认益处＋指出过度使用的代价＋设限制”的平衡结构。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
