## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

### 图意说明

一侧的父亲一边看电视一边要求孩子学习，另一侧的父亲自己坐下来与孩子一同学习。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

### 原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：教育者自己的行为具有示范作用，要求别人做到的事应先体现在自己的行动中。

审题提醒：不能把主题缩小为“不要看电视”；核心是言行一致与榜样作用。

### 合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> In the first picture, a father watches television while telling his son to study hard. In the second, the father reads beside the child, and both are concentrating on their books. The contrast shows that a good example can be more powerful than repeated instructions.
>
> Children learn not only from what adults say but also from what they do. If a parent asks for hard work while avoiding it personally, the message becomes less convincing. By contrast, shared effort makes the expected behavior clear and achievable. For example, parents who put away their phones during family reading time can help children develop better reading habits. Their actions also create a calmer environment for learning. This principle applies to teachers and leaders as well: responsibility begins with practicing what we ask of others.
>
> Therefore, adults should examine their own habits before simply blaming children. Setting aside regular time to read, keeping promises and following common rules are useful starting points. Words are still necessary, but they work best when supported by consistent action. Good education grows from both guidance and example.

## 三段怎样展开

1. 左图父亲看电视却要求孩子用功；右图父子一起学习。好榜样可能比反复要求更有效。

2. 孩子既听大人说，也看大人做。言行不一致削弱说服力，共同努力则使要求清楚可行；父母阅读时放下手机也能改善环境。

3. 大人先检查自己的习惯，再要求孩子。阅读、守信、守规则都是起点；言语指导与持续行动相配合。

## 模板

适用主题：亲情与教育。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

> The pictures show two ways of [guiding others]. In the first, [a person makes a demand without following it]. In the second, [the person sets an example]. The contrast highlights the influence of our own behavior.
>
> People learn from actions as well as words. When we ask others to [desired behavior] but fail to do it ourselves, our message may lose its power. By contrast, a clear example makes the expectation easier to understand and follow. For example, [an everyday action and its effect on others]. This is why those who guide others should first examine their own habits.
>
> Therefore, [responsible people] should practice what they ask of others. They can begin by [a small consistent action]. Advice and rules remain useful, but they are more convincing when supported by real behavior.

## 填槽说明

- guiding others 用动名词短语；如teaching children / leading a team。

- makes a demand 与sets an example 要体现同一要求的不同实现方式。

- 最后by 后接动名词，如keeping their own promises。

## 核心表达

convincing：有说服力的

achievable：能够做到的

consistent：一致的、持续稳定的

set an example：树立榜样

## 迁移方法

适用于父母教育、教师示范、领导责任和言行一致；不是所有亲子题都适合，2021重点为尊重兴趣。

## 一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
