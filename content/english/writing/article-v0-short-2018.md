## 题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：邀请。任务：给校内所有外籍专家发邮件，邀请参加毕业典礼；必须包含时间、地点和其他相关信息。约100词；署名Li Ming。

审题提醒：对象是多位专家；写清典礼安排即可，不要无依据地要求每位专家都作演讲。

### 合理补充的情境细节

6月25日9时、校礼堂、约两小时、发言顺序、合影及6月18日回复期限，均是范文设定；邀请对象是全体外籍专家。

## 范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

> Dear international experts,
>
> We would like to invite you to our graduation ceremony. It will be held in the university hall at 9 a.m. on June 25 and will last about two hours.
>
> The president will give a welcome speech, followed by speeches from teachers and student representatives. After the ceremony, there will be a group photo outside the hall. Your presence would mean a great deal to the graduates, whom you have supported during their studies.
>
> Please let us know by June 18 whether you can attend. We sincerely hope to share this special moment with you.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

> Dear [name or group],
>
> We would like to invite you to [event]. It will be held at [place] at [time] on [date] and will last about [duration].
>
> The event will begin with [first activity], followed by [second activity]. After that, there will be [final activity]. Your presence would mean a great deal to [people], and we hope you will enjoy the occasion.
>
> Please let us know by [reply date] whether you can attend. If you need any further information, please contact us. We sincerely hope to share this special moment with you.
>
> Yours sincerely,\
> Li Ming

## 中文段意

①邀请参加毕业典礼，说明地点、开始时间和持续时间。②介绍校长、教师和学生代表发言以及合影安排，说明专家出席对毕业生的意义。③请在指定日期前回复，希望共同分享这一特殊时刻。

## 填槽与改写

1. followed by 后接名词或动名词，不接完整的主谓句。

2. duration 填two hours；不要照搬本题长度到其他活动。

3. 群发称呼可用Dear guests / Dear colleagues；本题用international experts 对应对象。

## 核心表达

ceremony：典礼；representative：代表；presence：出席、在场；mean a great deal to：对⋯⋯意义重大。

## 迁移方法

适用于毕业典礼、庆祝会、开幕式等活动邀请。必须随任务说明“来做什么”，而非只背时间地点。
