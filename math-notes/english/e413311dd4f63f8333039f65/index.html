2023年英语一大作文｜传统文化在参与中延续

题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

图意说明

村里的龙舟比赛吸引许多观众，老人看到活动参与热情日益高涨。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：传统文化通过真实参与保持活力，也能连接不同年龄的人并增进社区联系。

审题提醒：图中没有统计图或旅游收入数据；不能无依据扩写为全国经济增长结论。

合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

The picture shows a lively dragon boat race in a village. Many people watch from a bridge and along the river, while two older villagers happily discuss how popular the event has become. The scene reflects the continuing appeal of a traditional cultural activity.

Traditions remain alive when people take part in them, rather than only reading about them. A dragon boat race brings neighbors together, allows younger people to learn from older generations and creates a shared sense of belonging. It can also make local history easier to understand through direct experience. For example, children who watch the race and hear its stories may become more interested in their community’s customs. The value of such events lies not only in excitement, but also in the relationships and memories they help build.

Therefore, communities should organize traditional activities in safe and welcoming ways. Schools and families can encourage young people to join and learn their meaning. By combining active participation with respect for tradition, we can keep cultural practices meaningful in modern life and pass them on to future generations.

三段怎样展开

  1. 村里龙舟赛热闹，桥上河边很多观众，老人高兴地谈论活动越来越受欢迎，体现传统活动的持续吸引力。

  2. 传统靠参与保持活力：邻里相聚、代际学习、形成归属感。儿童观看并听相关故事，也可能更理解社区习俗。

  3. 社区安全、开放地组织活动，学校家庭鼓励年轻人理解并参与，使传统在现代生活中保持意义、传给后代。

模板

适用主题：文化与社会。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

The picture shows [a traditional activity] attracting [participants or viewers]. [A visible detail] makes the scene lively and welcoming. It suggests that traditional culture can remain meaningful in modern life.

A tradition stays alive when people take part in it. Through [the activity], they can learn about [cultural knowledge], build relationships and develop a sense of belonging. For example, [a young person’s concrete experience and what it teaches]. Such events connect different generations and turn cultural knowledge into shared experience. Their value lies not only in entertainment but also in the memories and understanding they create.

Therefore, [a suitable organizer] should make the activity safe and accessible. Schools and families can help young people understand its meaning. With active participation and thoughtful support, we can pass useful traditions on to future generations.

填槽说明

  • visible detail 必须来自图，如crowds watching by the river。

  • tradition / activity 要与具体图一致：节日、戏曲、传统技艺均需改论据。

  • 不要只写protect culture；写清组织活动、解释意义、鼓励参与等实际做法。

核心表达

appeal：此处指“吸引力”

a sense of belonging：归属感

participation：参与；可用taking part

pass ... on：传承、传递

迁移方法

适用于传统节日、地方文化、传统艺术与社区认同。侧重“参与让传统延续”，不要照搬2010中外交流的全部内容。

一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
