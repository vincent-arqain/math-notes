题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

图意说明

毕业生站在分岔路口，面前有就业、继续深造、出国和创业等不同去向。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：面对多种人生道路，应结合自身能力、兴趣与实际条件作出选择，并承担后续行动。

审题提醒：图中没有宣布某条路最好；不能把考研写成唯一正确选择。

合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

The drawing shows a group of graduates standing at a crossroads. Several paths are open to them, including finding a job, further study, going abroad and starting a business. The scene reflects a question many young people face: which path should they choose?

There is no single answer that suits everyone. People differ in interests, abilities and personal conditions, so a popular choice is not necessarily the best one. Before making a decision, we need to understand ourselves and gather reliable information about the options. For example, a student who enjoys research may consider further study, while another may prefer to gain experience at work. Advice from others can be useful, but it should support our judgment rather than replace it. A thoughtful choice also requires preparation for possible difficulties.

Therefore, graduates should compare their options calmly and make a practical plan. Once a decision is made, they should take responsibility and work towards their goal, while remaining ready to adjust when necessary. What matters is choosing a suitable path and walking it with purpose.

三段怎样展开

  1. 毕业生站在求职、深造、出国、创业的岔路前，面对人生道路的选择。

  2. 没有适合所有人的同一个答案。要认识兴趣、能力和现实条件，收集信息，参考意见但保留判断，并准备面对困难。

  3. 冷静比较并制定计划；决定后负责任地行动，必要时调整。关键是选适合自己的路并认真走下去。

模板

适用主题：选择与学习。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

The drawing shows [people] facing several choices, such as [option one], [option two] and [option three]. It reflects a common question: how can we choose a path that is suitable for us?

There is no single answer for everyone. We differ in [personal factors], so following the crowd may not lead to a good result. Before deciding, we should understand our own needs and learn about each option. For example, [a comparison showing why different people may choose differently]. Advice can help, but the final decision should be based on careful thought. We should also consider the effort and difficulties that each choice involves.

Therefore, we need to compare our options and make a practical plan. After choosing, we should act responsibly and review our progress. A suitable choice becomes valuable when it is followed by steady effort.

填槽说明

  • people 为复数时use they；不要中途改成he。

  • personal factors 可填interests and abilities。

  • 例子用于说明因人而异，不把任何职业、学校或道路说成普遍优越。

核心表达

crossroads：十字路口；这里象征人生选择

option：可选方案

judgment：判断

adjust：调整

迁移方法

适用于职业、专业、毕业道路和人生规划。本题强调匹配与判断；2018选课更强调长期成长与眼前轻松的区别。

一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
