题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：建议。任务：代表学生会给即将来校的留学生写邮件：表示欢迎，并就校园生活提供建议。约100词；署名仍用Li Ming，不写地址。

审题提醒：必须同时完成“欢迎”和“建议”；代表学生会写信不等于把落款改成学生会。

合理补充的情境细节

加入社团、利用图书馆、向同学咨询课程是范文给出的建议，不是题面指定的三项活动。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

Dear international students,

On behalf of the Students’ Union, I would like to welcome you to our university. Here are some suggestions to help you settle in.

First, join a student club that matches your interests. It is an easy way to make friends and practice Chinese. Second, make good use of the library, where you can find useful books and quiet places to study. You may also ask your classmates for advice on courses.

Please feel free to contact us if you need help. We hope you will enjoy your studies and have a wonderful time on campus.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

Dear [name or group],

On behalf of [organization], I would like to welcome you to [place]. Here are some suggestions to help you settle in.

First, [action one]. This will help you [benefit one]. Second, [action two], where you can [useful activity]. It is also a good idea to [action three] when you have time.

Please feel free to contact us if you need help with [possible difficulty]. We will be happy to offer support. We hope you will enjoy your [studies / work] and have a wonderful time here.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

中文段意

①代表学生会欢迎大家，并提出帮助适应新环境的建议。②加入符合兴趣的社团，有助于交友和练习中文；使用图书馆，也可向同学咨询课程。③有需要可以联系学生会，祝大家在校学习和生活愉快。

填槽与改写

  1. action one、action two 用祈使句，如join a student club。

  2. where 必须紧接真实地点；若前句不是地点，就另起This will help you ...。

  3. 欢迎只是开头，正文仍应给出具体校园建议。

核心表达

on behalf of：代表；settle in：适应新环境；make good use of：充分利用；feel free to：尽管⋯⋯，请随时⋯⋯。

迁移方法

适用于新生欢迎、交流生生活建议和新员工适应建议。身份、建议场景与落款三处都要随题目调整。
