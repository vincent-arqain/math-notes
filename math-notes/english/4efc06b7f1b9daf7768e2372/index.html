题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

图意说明

两位学生看着讲座信息，一个因讲座不属于本专业而否定其价值，另一个愿意前去学习。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：不应仅以是否属于本专业判断知识价值，保持开放心态，主动接触不同领域。

审题提醒：不要写成只讨论“讲座好不好”。也不必照搬“肯定”作绝对保证：跨专业学习有价值，仍需合理安排时间。

合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

The picture shows two students reading a notice about a campus lecture. One thinks it is useless because it is outside their major, while the other believes attending would be helpful. Their disagreement raises a question about how widely we should learn.

Our major gives us useful knowledge, but it cannot cover every problem we may face. Ideas from other fields can broaden our understanding and offer new ways to think. For example, an engineering student may learn from a history lecture how technology changes people’s lives. Such learning may also improve communication with people from different backgrounds. This does not mean attending every event or neglecting our main studies. It means staying curious and recognizing that useful knowledge is not limited to the subjects listed in our own courses.

Therefore, students should sometimes explore a lecture, book or activity outside their major and think about what it can teach them. Universities can make these opportunities easier to find. With an open mind and sensible use of time, we can build a broader and more useful education.

三段怎样展开

  1. 两位学生对是否听非本专业讲座看法不同，提出学习范围应多宽的问题。

  2. 专业知识有用，但不能覆盖所有问题。其他领域能拓宽理解并帮助沟通；这不意味着场场参加或忽视本专业，而是保持好奇。

  3. 适当尝试专业外讲座、阅读或活动，学校提供便利。开放心态配合合理时间安排能带来更全面的教育。

模板

适用主题：选择与学习。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

The picture shows [people] discussing [a learning opportunity]. One rejects it because [a narrow reason], while the other sees possible value in it. This reminds us to keep an open mind towards learning.

Knowledge in [our familiar field] is important, but it does not answer every question. By exploring [another field], we may discover new ideas and improve [a useful ability]. For example, [a specific connection between two fields]. We can also learn to communicate with people whose experience differs from ours. Of course, broad learning should be balanced with our main responsibilities rather than becoming an excuse to avoid them.

Therefore, we should make time for suitable learning opportunities and reflect on what we gain. Schools can provide more chances to explore. Curiosity, combined with thoughtful choices, can make our education richer and more useful.

填槽说明

  • a narrow reason 填完整句，如it is outside their major。

  • 跨领域例子只需说清“学到什么”，无需编造名人事迹或研究数据。

  • combined with 后用名词，如thoughtful choices。

核心表达

major：此处指“专业”

broaden：拓宽

background：背景、经历

neglect：忽视

迁移方法

适用于开放学习、知识广度、终身学习和跨学科思维。与2018共用“学习收获”论点，本题特有的是专业边界。

一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
