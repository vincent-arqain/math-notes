题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：通知。任务：为研究生会写通知，为一场关于全球化的国际会议招募志愿者。应包括申请者基本条件及其他相关信息。约100词；必须署名Postgraduates’ Association。

审题提醒：不能署Li Ming；必须写基本资格。会议主题globalization 不可改成普通校园活动。

合理补充的情境细节

会议于6月12日至14日在本校举行、6月1日截止申请，以及迎宾、指路、会场准备、培训和申请办法，均是范文为成文所作的安排。全球化主题、研究生会身份及指定落款来自题意。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

Notice

The Postgraduates’ Association is looking for volunteers for an international conference on globalization. The conference will be held at our university from June 12 to 14.

Volunteers will welcome guests, give directions and help prepare meeting rooms. Applicants should speak English well, enjoy working with others and be free during the conference. Previous experience is welcome but not required. A short training session will be provided before the event.

If you are interested, please send a brief introduction of yourself to our office before June 1. Please include your contact details. We look forward to working with you.

Postgraduates’ Association

模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

Notice

[Organization] is looking for volunteers for [event]. The event will be held at [place] from [date] to [date].

Volunteers will [duty one] and [duty two]. Applicants should [skill requirement], enjoy working with others and be free during [required period]. Previous experience is welcome but not required. Training will be provided before the event.

If you are interested, please send [application materials] to [office or contact] before [deadline]. Please include your contact details. We look forward to working with you.

Postgraduates’ Association

中文段意

①研究生会为全球化主题国际会议招募志愿者，并说明会议时间地点。②介绍迎宾、指路、会场准备等职责，以及英语、合作和时间方面的条件；提供培训。③有意者按截止日期提交自我介绍和联系方式。

填槽与改写

  1. should 后接动词原形；写speak English well，不写fluent English 孤立短语。

  2. 日期与培训可合理补充，但不得遗漏题目指定的全球化会议。

  3. 完整机构落款Postgraduates’ Association 必须照题意保留。

核心表达

volunteer：志愿者；applicant：申请者；give directions：指路；previous experience：以往经验。

迁移方法

适用于会议、公益活动的志愿者招募。2023是招募一名研究助理，需要改人数，并把职责写成研究任务。
