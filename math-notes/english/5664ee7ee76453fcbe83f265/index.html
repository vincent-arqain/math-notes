题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：邀请。任务：给本校一位外教发邮件，邀请其担任即将举行的英语演讲比赛评委，并补充必要细节。约100词；署名Li Ming，不写地址。

审题提醒：邀请的是“担任评委”，不只是参加或发表演讲。日期、时间、地点和赛程可合理设定。

合理补充的情境细节

外教称作Professor Smith、5月15日14—16时、主礼堂、演讲主题、十位选手及5月8日回复期限，均为合理设定；担任评委是题目要求。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

Dear Professor Smith,

I am writing to invite you to serve as a judge for our English speech contest. It will take place in the main hall from 2 to 4 p.m. on May 15.

The topic is “My University Life,” and ten students will each give a short speech. We hope you can assess their performances and offer some advice at the end. Your experience in teaching English would be a great help to the students.

We would be very grateful if you could join us. Please let me know by May 8 whether you are available.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

Dear [name or group],

I am writing to invite you to serve as [role] for [event]. It will take place at [place] from [start time] to [end time] on [date].

The event will focus on [topic], and [participants] will [main activity]. We hope you can [specific duty] and offer some advice. Your experience in [field] would be a great help to us.

We would be very grateful if you could join us. Please let me know by [reply date] whether you are available. I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

中文段意

①邀请外教担任英语演讲比赛评委，并交代时间、地点。②十位学生围绕大学生活作简短演讲；请外教评价表现并给建议，说明邀请理由。③表达感谢，并请对方在指定日期前确认是否有空。

填槽与改写

  1. role 填a judge / a guest speaker；不要遗失a。

  2. 在地点前通常用at，具体日期前用on。

  3. specific duty 需符合角色；评委用assess the performances，嘉宾可用give a short talk。

核心表达

serve as：担任；assess：评价；可换成judge；performance：此处指“表现”；available：此处指“有空的”。

迁移方法

适用于邀请评委、专家或嘉宾。2022要求教授组织团队，必须把serve as a judge 改为organize a team。
