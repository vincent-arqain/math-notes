题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：通知。任务：为Smith 教授的校园体育活动研究项目招募一名学生，写通知说明工作职责和任职要求。约100词；署名Li Ming。

审题提醒：招募对象是a student，即一名学生；不是招运动员参加比赛。duties 和requirements 都必须明确。

合理补充的情境细节

准备问卷、访谈、整理资料、每周约四小时、5月20日前提交简历及后续面试，均是范文为职责、条件和申请流程补充的细节；Smith教授、校园体育研究、一名学生和Li Ming落款来自题意。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

Notice

One student is needed to assist Professor Smith with a research project on campus sports activities. This is a good chance to gain research experience.

The assistant will help prepare questionnaires, interview students and organize the information collected. Applicants should be interested in sports, communicate well and be able to use basic computer programs. Careful work and a strong sense of responsibility are also important. You should be available for about four hours a week.

If you are interested, please send a short CV to Professor Smith before May 20. Suitable applicants will be invited for an interview.

Li Ming

模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

Notice

One student is needed to assist [person] with a research project on [topic]. This is a good chance to gain experience.

The assistant will [duty one], [duty two] and [duty three]. Applicants should be interested in [field], communicate well and be able to [required skill]. Careful work and a strong sense of responsibility are also important. You should be available for [required time].

If you are interested, please send [materials] to [contact] before [deadline]. Suitable applicants will be invited for an interview.

Li Ming

中文段意

①为Smith 教授校园体育研究招一名助理，说明研究经历方面的价值。②职责是准备问卷、访谈和整理信息；条件包括兴趣、交流能力、基础电脑能力、认真负责及可投入的时间。③说明简历提交和后续面试。

填槽与改写

  1. 本题为One student is needed；若招募多人，再改为Several students are needed，并同步改写assistant。

  2. 职责用will do；资格用should be / be able to do，两类不能相互代替。

  3. 若不会questionnaire，用prepare survey questions（准备调查问题）即可。

核心表达

assist someone with：帮助某人处理⋯⋯；questionnaire：问卷；可替换为survey questions；sense of responsibility：责任感；suitable：合适的。

迁移方法

适用于研究助理、活动助理等招募。按“岗位＋职责＋资格＋申请方式”背诵，主题变了只换具体工作。
