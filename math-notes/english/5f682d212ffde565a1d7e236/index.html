题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

图意说明

两个学生处理任务的习惯不同：一个倾向提前完成，另一个总等到最后才开始。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：不同做事习惯影响时间安排与完成质量；及早开始优于习惯性拖延。

审题提醒：不要仅写“习惯很重要”；需点明及时完成与拖到最后的对比。不能把尽早完成解释为赶工。

合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

The pictures show two different working habits. One student prefers to finish tasks early, while the other waits until the last moment to begin. Although both have work to do, their attitudes towards time are very different.

This contrast suggests that good habits can make a real difference to our lives. Starting early gives us enough time to understand a task, solve problems and check mistakes. It also reduces the pressure caused by an approaching deadline. Delaying everything, on the other hand, may turn a simple task into a stressful experience. For example, preparing for an exam a little each day usually leaves more time for review than trying to learn everything in one night. Good planning supports careful work rather than unnecessary speed.

To develop better habits, we should divide large tasks into small ones and decide when to begin each step. Putting away our phones for a short study period can also help. We do not need to finish everything immediately, but we should avoid making delay our normal way of life.

三段怎样展开

  1. 两图呈现两种做事习惯：尽早完成与拖到最后才开始，区别在于时间态度。

  2. 早开始有时间理解、解决问题、检查错误，也减少截止日期带来的压力。每天准备一点通常优于临时突击；好计划支持认真工作而非盲目赶快。

  3. 把大任务拆小、安排开始时间、暂时放下手机。无需立刻做完所有事，但不要让拖延成为常态。

模板

适用主题：态度与行动。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

The pictures present two different habits. One person [a timely action], while the other [a delaying action]. The contrast shows how our everyday habits may influence the way we complete our work.

A good habit helps us [benefit one] and [benefit two]. When we [a positive action], we have more time to deal with unexpected problems. By contrast, [a bad habit] may lead to [a direct consequence]. For example, [a familiar case from study or work]. This suggests that effective planning is about making steady progress, not simply doing everything faster.

To improve, we can divide tasks into small steps and set a starting time for each one. We should also [a practical way to reduce distraction]. With regular practice, useful actions can become habits that support our long-term goals.

填槽说明

  • bad habit 用动名词，如putting off work；后接may lead to。

  • consequence 用名词短语，如avoidable mistakes / unnecessary pressure。

  • 好习惯需写出实际步骤：拆任务、规定开始时间、减少干扰。

核心表达

deadline：截止日期

delay：推迟、拖延

stressful：令人紧张的

rather than：而不是

迁移方法

适用于时间管理、自律和学习习惯。若图中是运动或环保习惯，需替换具体利弊，不能保留考试复习的全部论证。

一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
