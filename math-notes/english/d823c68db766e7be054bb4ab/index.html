题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

类型：邀请。任务：给英国一所大学的教授发邮件，邀请其组织团队参加将在你校举行的国际创新大赛。约100词；署名Li Ming。

审题提醒：关键词是organize a team：邀请教授组队，不是让教授当评委，也不只是请教授本人出席。

合理补充的情境细节

Professor Smith这一称呼、7月10日至11日、每队三至五名学生和一位教师、作品形式及6月10日回复期限，均是范文补充；请教授组织团队参赛是题目要求。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

Dear Professor Smith,

I am writing to invite you to organize a team for the International Innovation Contest at our university. The contest will be held on July 10 and 11.

Each team may include three to five students and a teacher. Participants will present a project that uses new ideas to solve a problem in daily life. We believe your students would bring valuable ideas and enjoy exchanging views with teams from other countries.

Please let us know by June 10 if you are interested. We will then send you the application form and further information. We look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

模板

对应原稿模板；方括号中的词和备选项须按新题替换。

Dear [name or group],

I am writing to invite you to organize a team for [contest] at [host institution]. The contest will be held on [date].

Each team may include [team members]. Participants will [main task], and they will have a chance to [benefit]. We believe your students would bring valuable ideas and learn from other teams.

Please let us know by [reply date] if you are interested. We will then send you [materials] and further information about [important detail]. We hope your university can take part and look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

中文段意

①邀请英国教授组织团队参加本校国际创新大赛，并说明日期。②说明示例组队人数与展示内容：用新想法解决日常问题；强调国际交流价值。③请有意者在截止日前回复，之后发送申请表和更多信息。

填槽与改写

  1. main task 填present a project / solve a practical problem。

  2. 组队人数、日期均是范文的合理设定，不是原题给定事实。

  3. interest 后不能直接接本模板；保留if you are interested 的完整结构。

核心表达

innovation：创新；participant：参与者；exchange views：交流观点；application form：申请表。

迁移方法

适用于邀请组队参赛、校际合作活动。与2013、2018共用“邀请＋安排＋回复”，但核心动作必须改为组织团队。
