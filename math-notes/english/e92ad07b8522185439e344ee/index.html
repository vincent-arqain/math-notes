题意概述

以下为讲义对历年真题要求的中文转述，不是题面逐字引文。

按本册说明，本题要求描述图画、解释寓意并作评论，按160—200词练习。

图意说明

登山途中，一人因疲惫而不想继续，同伴递上饮品并劝他先休息、再向前走。

以上文字是网页版自行撰写的图意说明，不展示原题插图，也不是原图题注的逐字转录。

原稿立意与审题提醒

立意：面对困难不轻易放弃；休息、调整和伙伴鼓励能帮助人坚持。

审题提醒：坚持是主线，合作是辅助；不要写成“永不休息”或只泛谈友谊。

合理补充的情境细节

范文分析段中的生活例子、原因推演及结尾提出的行动是原创论证内容，不是图中事实或题面附加条件；应与上面的图意说明分开理解。

范文

原创练习答案，保留原稿正文。

The picture shows two people on a mountain path. One sits down and says that he is too tired to continue. His companion offers him a drink and suggests taking a rest before moving on. The scene reminds us of the value of not giving up.

Any meaningful goal usually takes time and effort. When progress is slow, we may feel like stopping, just as the tired climber does. Yet a temporary difficulty does not mean that the goal is beyond our reach. A short rest, a better method or encouragement from a friend can help us continue. For instance, a student who struggles with English can reduce the daily task and keep practicing, instead of abandoning the subject completely. Small steps, repeated over time, can produce real improvement.

We should therefore learn to continue in a sensible way. Set a clear goal, divide it into manageable tasks and seek help when necessary. Persistence is not working without rest. It is returning to the task after a pause and moving steadily towards the goal.

三段怎样展开

  1. 两人登山，一人疲惫想放弃，同伴递饮品并建议休息后继续。这提醒我们不要轻易放弃。

  2. 有意义的目标需要时间和努力；暂时困难不意味着目标无法达到。休息、改进方法和鼓励都有帮助。英语学习困难时可缩小每日任务并持续练习。

  3. 合理地坚持：明确目标、拆分任务、需要时求助。坚持并非不休息，而是休息后回来继续。

模板

适用主题：态度与行动。本模板保留本题的论证路线；方括号内的内容需要随新图替换，并用具体例子补足至160～200词。

In the picture, [a person] is trying to [goal] but wants to stop because of [difficulty]. Another person [helpful response]. The scene shows that reaching a goal often requires continued effort and suitable support.

When we work towards [a meaningful goal], difficulties are unavoidable. However, one setback does not prove that we are unable to succeed. By [a useful adjustment], we can make the task easier to continue. For example, [a concrete case of gradual progress]. Support from others can also help us regain confidence. What matters is to return to the task instead of giving it up completely.

Therefore, we should set realistic goals and divide them into small steps. We should also [a suitable form of rest or help]. Continued effort, together with a sensible method, makes progress more likely.

填槽说明

  • By 后用动名词：changing our method / taking a short break。

  • 范文的合理休息来自原图；若另一题强调坚持但没有休息情节，可写调整学习方法。

  • 结尾同时交代“继续努力”和“如何继续”，避免重复口号。

核心表达

temporary：暂时的

beyond our reach：超出能力范围／难以达到

manageable：可以应付的；可换成small

persistence：坚持；不熟时用not giving up

迁移方法

适用坚持、克服困难和长期目标；可与2012的积极态度结合，但须保留本题的行动过程。

一分钟复述

合上范文，用中文各说一句：图中最关键的细节是什么？它通过什么过程影响人或环境？我能提出哪两项具体行动？再把这三句扩成英文三段，检查是否真正回应了图画。
